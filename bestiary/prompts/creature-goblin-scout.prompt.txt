Character design of a fantasy Goblin Scout. Clear clean lines, European comic book style, ligne claire, ink outline, flat colors with limited muted palette and minimal cell shading, Hergé and Moebius style influence. The goblin is wearing leather armor, holding a small scimitar, and scouting from behind a forest bush. Alpha-transparent background. No gradients, no messy crosshatching. Aspect ratio approximately 1:1.
