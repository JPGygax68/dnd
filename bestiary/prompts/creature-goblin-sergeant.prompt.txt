Character design of a fantasy Goblin Sergeant. Clear clean lines, European comic book style, ligne claire, ink outline, flat colors with limited muted palette and minimal cell shading, Hergé and Moebius style influence. The goblin sergeant is a battle-ready leader wearing scale armor, carrying a spear and a horn, standing in a commanding pose. Clean solid background. No gradients, no messy crosshatching. Aspect ratio 2:3.
