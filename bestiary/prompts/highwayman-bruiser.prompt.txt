Character design of a fantasy human bandit (highwayman).
Clear clean lines, European comic book style, ligne claire, ink outline, flat colors with limited muted palette and minimal cell shading, Hergé and Moebius style influence. 
The highwayman is the thug of a duo (leader not shown here), wearing a leather jacket, armed with a wooden club.
He is short and stocky, wearing a grim but somewhat vacant expression on his chubby face.
Background shows a bit of road and some trees, simplified and with weaker color tones. No background color (alpha transparent). Very simplified gradients, no messy crosshatching.
